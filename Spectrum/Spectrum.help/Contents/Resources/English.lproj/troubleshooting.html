Troubleshooting

The header says Stopped while a source is selected

That is expected when the source is silent — for example Spotify or Music is paused, or the microphone is quiet. Spectrum stays attached to the input and shows Running again as soon as audio arrives. The live bars clear and the peak-hold markers fall to the floor, then the plot rests until audio returns.

The plot is flat or stuck at the dB floor

  1. Select Test Signal > Sine 1 kHz. If you see a peak, the analyzer works and the live source is silent or blocked.
  2. For a microphone, confirm Microphone is granted and the correct device is selected. Speak or play into that input.
  3. For System Audio or an application, follow Screen Recording: enable Spectrum, quit completely, open Spectrum again, then play audio.
  4. Application capture lists only apps that are currently outputting audio. Open the Input menu again after playback starts.
  5. Check the dB range in Settings. If the floor is very high, quiet signals disappear.

System Settings says Spectrum is allowed, but system audio is still silent

Quit Spectrum completely (Command-Q), then open it again and choose System Audio or an application while something is playing. macOS does not apply a new Screen Recording grant until the app is restarted. If the Screen Recording list shows more than one Spectrum, turn them on, quit, and reopen the app. See Quit and reopen after granting access.

macOS asked for Screen Recording and I am worried about privacy

That prompt is required so Spectrum can hear what other apps are playing. It does not record the screen. Read Privacy & permissions.

I turned on Accessibility and nothing changed

Spectrum does not use the Accessibility permission. Use Microphone and/or Screen Recording instead.

Nyquist limited

Your maximum frequency is above half the sample rate. Lower the maximum in Settings or use a higher-rate device. The extra range cannot contain real signal.

High CPU or low FPS

Choose the Low CPU preset, fewer bars, a smaller FFT, 50% overlap, or 30 fps. The status bar FPS is the real update rate.

Device unplugged

Spectrum reports a disconnected input and switches to another device or the 1 kHz test signal.
