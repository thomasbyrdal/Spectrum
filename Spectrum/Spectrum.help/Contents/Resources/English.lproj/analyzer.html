Analyzer settings

Bars, FFT size, and ranges can be changed from the control bar or from Spectrum > Settings… > Spectrum.

Bar count

Each bar is a log-spaced frequency band. More bars show more detail and cost more drawing work. Typical values are 64–256; 1024 is for very dense displays.

FFT size

The FFT length in samples. Frequency resolution is sample rate ÷ FFT size. At 48 kHz:

FFT sizeBin spacing (approx.)
204823.4 Hz
409611.7 Hz
81925.9 Hz
163842.9 Hz
327681.5 Hz
655360.73 Hz

Low frequencies (for example near 20–80 Hz) need a large FFT or they smear across bars. Larger FFTs are heavier on the CPU. Presets: Speech uses a mid-size FFT and 80 Hz–8 kHz; Music and Detailed use larger FFTs; Low CPU uses a smaller FFT and 30 fps.

Window

  • Hann — default; good general-purpose leakage control
  • Blackman-Harris — lower sidelobes, slightly wider main lobe
  • Rectangular — no taper; sharpest peaks, more leakage into neighboring bins

Frequency range

Default display span is 20 Hz to 22 500 Hz. Settings > Spectrum lets you edit minimum and maximum Hz. The right edge is clamped to Nyquist (half the input sample rate). At 44.1 kHz, you cannot display 22.5 kHz; the chip shows Nyquist limited.

dB range

Vertical scale in dBFS. Default floor is −100 dB. The top of the scale is 0 dB in Settings unless you lower it. Signals below the floor appear empty; signals above the top clip to the top of the plot.
