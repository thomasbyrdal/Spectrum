Appearance

Bar colors and peak hold are on the control bar (Style and Peak) and in Settings > Appearance.

Bar style

  • Solid — a single teal fill
  • Gradient — rainbow from low (cool) to high (warm) frequencies
  • Blue gradient — blue family, darker at the base of each bar
  • Green gradient — green family
  • Red gradient — red family
  • Heat — color follows level, not frequency: blue when quiet, then green and yellow, then orange and red at the top of the dB range

The theme is Classic Dark. Frequency and dB grid lines can be turned off in Settings.

Stereo layout

When Show stereo spectrum is on, Settings > Appearance > Layout chooses how the two analyzers are arranged:

  • Left | Right — left channel on the left, right channel on the right. This is the default.
  • Right over left — right channel on top, left channel on the bottom.

The choice is remembered the next time you open the app.

Glow and reflection

In Settings > Appearance:

  • Bar glow — a soft bloom around louder bars. On by default. Quiet bars stay dark so the plot does not haze.
  • Mirrored reflection — a faint flipped copy of each bar just above the frequency axis. Off by default so it does not compete with dB reading.

Peak hold

Peak hold remembers the recent maximum in each bar, then decays. Hold time is set in Settings (about 0.4–3 seconds).

  • Off — no peak graphics
  • Bar — a light marker at the held peak
  • Rect — a darker rectangle in the same color family as the bar, drawn only in the gap between the live bar and the held peak, falling with the peak
