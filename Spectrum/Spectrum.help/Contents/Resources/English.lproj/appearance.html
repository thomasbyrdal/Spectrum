Appearance

Bar colors and peak hold are on the control bar (Style and Peak) and in Settings > Appearance.

Bar style

  • Solid — a single teal fill
  • Gradient — rainbow from low (cool) to high (warm) frequencies
  • Blue gradient — blue family, darker at the base of each bar
  • Green gradient — green family
  • Red gradient — red family

The theme is Classic Dark. Frequency and dB grid lines can be turned off in Settings.

Peak hold

Peak hold remembers the recent maximum in each bar, then decays. Hold time is set in Settings (about 0.4–3 seconds).

  • Off — no peak graphics
  • Bar — a light marker at the held peak
  • Rect — a darker rectangle in the same color family as the bar, drawn only in the gap between the live bar and the held peak, falling with the peak
