Spectrum Help

Spectrum is a real-time frequency analyzer for macOS. It listens to audio and draws a live spectrum. It does not record audio to disk, and it does not record your screen.

System Audio and application capture need Screen Recording permission. macOS uses that privacy switch so Spectrum can hear playback. Spectrum never captures video, screenshots, or window contents. See Privacy & permissions for the full explanation and setup steps.

What Spectrum does

Choose an input, and Spectrum performs a fast Fourier transform (FFT) on the incoming audio, then draws frequency bars from low to high. You can analyze:

  • Built-in test signals (no permission required)
  • Physical inputs such as microphones and audio interfaces (Microphone permission)
  • Everything playing through the Mac’s output, or a single application (Screen Recording permission for audio only)

If the input is Spotify or Apple Music, you can also control playback from Spectrum: Play, Stop, Next, and Previous, plus the current song and artist in the header and window title. macOS may ask for Automation permission for that app the first time.
